# Privacy Policy for [REALISM Lux Green]

**Last Updated:** 2026-10-07

This Privacy Policy applies to the watch face application **[REALISM Lux Green]** (hereinafter referred to as "the App") created by **[SoftPower]**.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The App does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, sensitive user data, or location tracking information. 

All display features (such as time, date, battery status, step counts, and complications) function strictly on-device using official Wear OS system APIs and are not transmitted to external servers.

## 2. Permissions
The App may require basic sensors or body sensors permissions solely for the purpose of displaying real-time metrics (e.g., heart rate or step count) directly on your smartwatch screen. No data accessed through these permissions is recorded or stored externally.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs that collect user information.
